Welcome to my academic homepage. I am Laibin Chang, a Ph.D. candidate in Software Engineering at Wuhan University (WHU). I am currently affiliated with the [SIGMA Lab](https://sigma.whu.edu.cn/index.htm), supervised by [Prof. Bo Du](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Shy1gnMAAAAJ) and Prof. [Prof. Chang Xu](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=N4F_3eoAAAAJ). Before that, I obtained my M.Eng. and B.Eng. degrees in Information and Communication Engineering and Electronic Information Engineering from China University of Petroleum (East China), advised by Assoc. Prof. Huajun Song and [Prof. Peng Ren](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=84q_1TEAAAAJ).

My research focuses on computer vision and intelligent information processing, with an emphasis on challenging low-quality and complex environments. I work on image enhancement, underwater image processing, image segmentation, multimodal fusion, and multi-task learning. I have published several first-author papers in top-tier venues such as *IEEE TPAMI*, *AAAI*, *IEEE TMM*, *ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing*, and *Neural Networks*, and I actively collaborate with Prof. Bo Du, Prof. Chang Xu, and other researchers in the field.

My research interests include:

• **Low-level Vision & Image Enhancement:** underwater image restoration, color correction, diffusion-based enhancement, visibility-to-recognizability modeling  
• **Image Segmentation & Recognition:** fine-grained segmentation, marine saliency detection, multimodal segmentation  
• **Multimodal Learning:** cross-modal fusion, diffusion models, prompt-based segmentation (e.g., SAM adaptation)

📬 I am open to collaboration and welcome inquiries from anyone interested in my research. Please feel free to contact me.
